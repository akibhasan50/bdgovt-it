**Q1: Choose the correct sentence:**

- **(A)** Rahim is as tall as mine
- **(B)** Rahim is tall as mine
- **(C)** Rahim is as tall as I
- **(D)** Rahim is as tall as me

**Answer:** **(C) Rahim is as tall as I**

**Q2: There is ______ on the roads today.**

- **(A)** Few traffics
- **(B)** Very much traffic
- **(C)** Too much traffic
- **(D)** Too many traffic

**Answer:** **(C) Too much traffic**

**Q3: A tank is 40% full. If 16 liters of water is added to the tank, it becomes 4/5 full. The capacity of the tank is:**

- **(A)** 32 liters
- **(B)** 35 liters
- **(C)** 40 liters
- **(D)** 42 liters

**Answer:** **(C) 40 liters**
