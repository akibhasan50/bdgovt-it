**Q25: If log₄ x = 12, then log₂(x / 4) = ?**

- **(A)** 11
- **(B)** 22
- **(C)** 44
- **(D)** 2

**Answer:** **(B) 22**

**Explanation:** log₄ x = 12 ⇒ x = 4¹². Then log₂(x/4) = log₂(4¹² / 4) = log₂(4¹¹) = 2 × 11 = 22.
