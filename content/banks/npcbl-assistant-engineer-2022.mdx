## Multiple choice questions (Q1–Q10)

**Q1: Who was the commander in chief of the mukti bahini?**

- **(A)** M.A. Rab
- **(B)** M.A.G Osmani
- **(C)** K.M Shafiullah
- **(D)** A.K Khander

**Answer:** **(B) M.A.G Osmani**

**Q2: Total amount of budget of Bangladesh for FY 2022-2023 was-**

- **(A)** 6,78,064 Cr, TK
- **(B)** 5,23,190 Cr, TK
- **(C)** 6,80,473 Cr, TK
- **(D)** 7,80,064 Cr, TK

**Answer:** **(A) 6,78,064 Cr, TK**

**Q3: What is the current rank of Bangladesh of SDG?**

- **(A)** 109
- **(B)** 104
- **(C)** 136
- **(D)** 129

**Answer:** **(B) 104**
